## Theoretical Foundation

band_frac(X) = ∫_{0.3·X_max}^{0.7·X_max} p_X(x) dx

In the large-sample limit (X_max → 1):
band_frac(X) ≈ ∫_{0.3}^{0.7} p_X(x) dx
